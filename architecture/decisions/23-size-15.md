# 23. Size 15 in the game and the library

- **Status:** done
- **Date:** 2026-11-17 (`399d3d5` and the commit that adds the puzzles)

The browser game goes up to 15×15 (new puzzle, pasted puzzles, parties,
library, designer): `web.MaxN` 14 → 15. The text format would allow up to 52
(`puzzle.MaxN`), the board would not: 15 is the limit on purpose, because the
cells get very small on a phone.

What made it possible is the bitmask brute force (`91355ac`): the generator
checks uniqueness with it ([5](05-generator-uses-brute-force.md)), and before
it 14×14 took seconds and larger sizes far longer. Measured on an otherwise
idle machine (16 cores):

- `generate -n 15`, 200 seeds: median 0.03 s, at most 0.16 s
  (N = 14: 0.02 / 0.04 s). So the pool needs no special handling for 15.
- Designer completions (`generator.Complete`, 12 disc patterns and 6 with two
  large rectangular blocks per size): N = 10 to 14 mean 0.02 to 0.16 s, worst
  1.6 s; N = 15 mean 1.4 s, worst 12 s, 1 of 18 failed (attempt limit). The
  blocks, once the slow case, now take under 0.8 s.

## Hard puzzles of size 15

18 new puzzles in `testdata/hard` (now 193, 162 of them need lookahead even
with all rules), a third of the 53 of size 14, as asked. Search with
`starbattle hardsearch -n 15 -min-lookahead 4` at commit `399d3d5`, same
threshold as [21](21-library-175.md).

- Searched seeds 1 to 2543 (stopped by hand): base rules stuck 348, lookahead
  with all rules 65 (one in 39, a little more often than at N = 14), at least
  4 lookahead steps 39 (one in 65). Guessing was never needed. Taken over are
  the first 18 in seed order (seeds 35 to 1082), so the searched range stays
  one block; the 21 later hits were dropped and are found again by searching
  from 1083 on. Wall time of the search: about 3 minutes.
- Ratings ([20](20-difficulty-rating.md)): 9 × 2 stars, 9 × 3 stars. The
  library now has 31 / 82 / 80 puzzles with 1 / 2 / 3 stars.
- Longest chains: `n15_s182` (40 steps), `n15_s053` (32), `n15_s1068` (30),
  `n15_s1082` (21). Longest thought experiment 7 (`n15_s035`, `n15_s182`),
  below the 9 of `n13_s4661`.
- Solving time, one puzzle at a time (`starbattle solve -runner sync`): 0.3 to
  10.6 s, all 18 together 50 s. `n15_s1068` (10.6 s) is the second slowest of
  the library after `n14_s1262` (13.0 s). The times the search prints go up to
  71 s, because it solves 16 seeds at once.
- The whole library one by one: 254 s (was 202 s, [22](22-ci-sample-only.md)),
  1 star 0.05 s, 2 stars 52 s, 3 stars 202 s.

## Sample

`sample.idx` grows from 27 to 29: `n15_s485` (2 stars, 4 steps, the cheapest
of the size, 0.3 s) and `n15_s130` (3 stars, 14 steps, 0.9 s). Solving the
sample takes 20.7 s instead of 20 s. The focused tests without `-short`
(`TestHardPuzzles` 53 → 55 s, `TestPriorityOrder` 13 → 14.5 s, `TestJSLogic`
27.6 → 28.7 s, `TestLibraryRatings` 29 → 30 s) grow by 1 to 2 s each;
`go test -short ./...` takes 25 s. The full run with `STARBATTLE_ALL_HARD=1`
should grow with the library's solving time (×1.26), to about twelve minutes
locally and 45 in CI; estimated, not measured.
